# Sales Rep Agent

Sales Rep Agent takes on the administrative work around a sales call. It researches accounts before a conversation, writes up the CRM after it, answers CRM and knowledge questions in chat, and flags deals that need attention between calls.

## What Sales Rep Agent does

Sales Rep Agent supports sales teams across the deal lifecycle:

- **Call completion workflow**: Summarizes a completed call against your selling methodology, updates the opportunity after you approve the changes, and prepares a note, a follow-up task, and a drafted follow-up email, if enabled
- **Account research and Customer 360**: Consolidates CRM, support, document, calendar, enrichment, and web data into one account summary from a single request
- **CRM self-service operations**: Finds, creates, and updates CRM records in conversation, with confirmation before any change
- **CRM alerts and notifications**: Flags stalled deals, missing stage-required fields, and passed close dates, and escalates alerts that go unaddressed
- **Knowledge base retrieval**: Answers questions from connected sales content, such as battlecards, case studies, and internal policies

## Connected systems

Sales Rep Agent connects to the following applications:

- **CRM**: Salesforce
- **Call recording**: Gong, Zoom
- **Enrichment**: ZoomInfo
- **Support and ITSM**: Freshdesk, Jira Service Desk
- **Calendar**: Google Calendar
- **Email**: Gmail
- **Knowledge sources**: Confluence, Freshdesk, Gong, Google Drive, Jira Service Desk, Microsoft SharePoint, Salesforce, Salesforce Service Cloud, Slack
- **Chat interface**: Slack
