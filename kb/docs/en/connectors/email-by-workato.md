# Email by Workato

Email by Workato is designed to allow users to easily send internal notifications for low-volume use cases around critical recipe alerts, debugging, or testing. You can also use this connector to quickly build proof of concepts before migrating to a production-ready email service provider.

> **EMAIL FORMAT**
> 
> Workato appends a footer to all emails sent with the **Send email** action: `This email was generated by Workato on behalf of <ACME_CORP>`. The `<ACME_CORP>` value uses your [workspace name](/en/workspace#workspace), or, if not configured, the workspace admin's username.
> 
> In non-production environments, the footer includes the environment type: `This email was generated by Workato on behalf of Example Org (Development).`
> 
> The sender address is `mailer@workato.com` by default.

> **FEATURE CONSIDERATIONS**
> 
> [Email by Workato](/en/connectors/email-by-workato#email-by-workato) connector capabilities for workspaces in the China data center are supported through the following channels only:
> 
> - Custom SMTP configuration.
> - Out-of-the-box Workato notifications. This includes error notifications, billing and usage alerts, and platform communications such as workspace sign-ups and password resets.
> 
> This reflects local regulatory requirements and applies to our multi-tenant and [Virtual Private Workato (VPW)](/en/cloud-hosting-editions/hosting-editions#virtual-private-workato) offerings.

## Send Email action

### Input

| Input | Description |
| --- | --- |
| To | Enter the email address of the recipient. |
| Subject | Enter the subject of the email to send. |
| Message | Enter the content of the email to send. It is HTML formatted if the Email type is set to HTML. |
| Attachments | Input the attachments to be sent along with the email. |
| Email Type | Select the format of the email message. Defaults to HTML. |
| Bcc | Enter the email address of the recipient to be sent a "Blind carbon copy". |
| Cc | Enter the email address of the recipient to be sent a "Carbon copy". |

### Output

| Output | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Remaining calls | The number of remaining calls Workato can execute out of the overall daily quota (500 calls). |
| Consumed calls | The number of actions completed out of the overall daily quota (500 calls). |
| Reset time | The time the daily quota resets. |

## Quotas

Email by Workato is subject to the following rate limits:

- 500 emails for each workspace in a 24-hour period

If you exceed this rate limit, Workato throws an error and fails the job. If you need to send emails at scale we recommend utilizing you own email provider like Outlook, Gmail, Mailchimp, Sendgrid, and more. To help you keep track of your usage, Workato supplies output datapills corresponding to your current consumed calls and reset time for the quotas.

# Troubleshoot Email by Workato runtime errors

Use this guide to troubleshoot errors that occur at runtime when you use the Email by Workato connector in Workato.

## Email delivery delays

Recipients may receive emails sent through Email by Workato with delays up to 24 hours. Workato logs show on-time delivery and message headers confirm the correct send time, but the delay occurs during email infrastructure routing. This error occurs because Email by Workato is not meant for use cases with a high volume of email messages.

Complete the following steps to troubleshoot this issue:

1. Whitelist all required IP addresses on your server and any partner servers. Refer to [IP allowlists](/en/security/ip-allowlists#ip-allowlists) for the complete list of Workato IP addresses.
   
2. Verify that any anti-spam solutions have [mailer@workato.com](mailto:mailer@workato.com) marked as a safe sender.
   
3. Use an external email provider and it's corresponding Workato connector if delays persist. Other email providers may perform better for high-volume email scenarios.
   

## Job is marked as complete but the recipient did not receive the email

This error occurs when a job is marked as complete in the job report, but the recipient did not receive the email sent in the recipe workflow.

Complete the following troubleshooting steps to resolve this error:

1. Check your spam folders to ensure that the email wasn't sent there.
   
2. Send a test email to the recipient's email address to ensure that the email address is valid.
   
3. [Contact Workato support](https://support.workato.com/en/support/home) to check if the email is blocked by Workato if the preceding steps didn't work.
